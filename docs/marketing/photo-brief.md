# MyTrackYo Photography Brief

Commission warm, documentary-style photography in real independent hospitality settings. Use natural light, real working details, and unposed moments. Do not imply that an image shows a MyTrackYo customer or property unless that relationship is confirmed and released.

1. Front-desk hands placing room keys out for an arrival, morning light.
2. Owner on a phone with a paper notebook and tablet visible, at the property desk.
3. Homestay host checking a booking on a phone in a courtyard.
4. Tea or coffee preparation before guests arrive.
5. Hostel common room during an ordinary midday moment.
6. Property operator reviewing a handwritten balance note at a desk at dusk.

Avoid stock-corporate teams, laptop-pointing, boardrooms, forced smiles, staged customer portraits, and generated signage. Crop for a human working detail and keep text overlays on calm, high-contrast surfaces. Current repository photography is not used as documentary evidence because its provenance and people/context could not be verified.
